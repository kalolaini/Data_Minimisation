How it works

An interactive assessment across five critical areas.

The platform guides you through practical questions and delivers tailored recommendations based on your current data practices.

01

Data collection

What data are you gathering from patients, and is every field truly necessary for care?

02

Data sources

Where does patient information come from, and which third parties feed data into your systems?

03

Purpose test

Do you actually need all this data for patient care, or can some fields be challenged and removed?

04

Storage and protection

How and where are you keeping patient data, and are your safeguards proportionate to its sensitivity?

05

Retention and disposal

How long are you holding data, and do you have policies to delete it once its purpose is served?

Regulatory alignment

Grounded in frameworks relevant to healthcare providers.

Recommendations are based on privacy and security frameworks commonly used to assess responsible data handling.

Australian Privacy Principles

The core privacy obligations under the Privacy Act 1988 for health service providers.

GDPR

Relevant where organisations handle data of EU residents and need strong minimisation controls.

My Health Record

My Health Record is a secure online summary of your key health information, accessible by you and authorized healthcare providers anytime to support safe, coordinated care.

ISO standards

A risk-based approach to information security management and governance.
